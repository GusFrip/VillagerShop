# VillagerShop 1.4.3 — changelog for the CurseForge file upload

**Cross-mod: PillagerControl support** (all of it is inert without PillagerControl installed)

- The shop vendor can now be any mob, not just a villager: with **PillagerControl** installed, a pacified pillager can run your counter (shopkeeper outfit included on their side)
- New **Guard Kit upgrade slot** in the Upgrades tab — only visible when a pillager runs the shop. Install PillagerControl's Guard Kit and your vendor shoots back with his crossbow when attacked (never targets players or villagers)
- **Immortality (Nether Star) is now compatible with self-defense**: an armed immortal vendor takes no damage but still knows who attacked him
- The **Movement upgrade** (prismarine/amethyst) releases the vendor while he walks to his Mess for his daily meal, then takes over again
- Guide book: new conditional chapter "A pillager behind the counter" (EN/FR), shown only when PillagerControl is installed

**Fixes**

- Shift-clicking a Guard Kit no longer swallows the item into a hidden slot when a villager runs the shop
- Locate button works with non-villager vendors
